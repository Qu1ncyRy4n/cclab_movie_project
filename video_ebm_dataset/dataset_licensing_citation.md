# Dataset Licensing & Citation

These videos are freely available for research purposes, but please do not share
them yourself. If a colleague is interested in using them, have them contact
**Eliza Bliss-Moreau** (eblissmoreau@ucdavis.edu). The lab tracks which groups
are using the video set to report to funders.

## Required citations

All presentations and publications using these videos must cite both papers:

> Bliss-Moreau, E., Machado, C.J., & Amaral, D.G. (2013). Macaque cardiac
> physiology is sensitive to the valence of passively viewed sensory stimuli.
> *PLoS One*, 8(8), e71170.

> Machado, C.J., Bliss-Moreau, E., Platt, M., & Amaral D.G. (2011). Social and
> nonsocial content differentially modulates visual attention and autonomic arousal
> in rhesus macaques. *PLoS One*, 6(10): e26598.

## Video content notation

Two rating systems are available (see CSVs in this folder):

| File | Paper | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `Machado et al. 2011 Video Content.csv` | Machado et al. 2011 | Broad content categories per video |
| `Bliss-Moreau, Machado, & Amaral, 2013 Video Rating.csv` | Bliss-Moreau et al. 2013 | Human ratings of psychological properties (valence, dominance, arousal, etc.) |
